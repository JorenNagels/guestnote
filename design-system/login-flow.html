Prototype for .impeccable/surfaces/src-app-pro-public-login.md. Nothing here ships. Every colour is read from the real design-system/tokens.css, so what you see is the shipping token, not an approximation. The ground steps one stop deeper down the neutral ramp at each rung completed — the passkey path skips the middle rung entirely, which is what “primary” should feel like: visibly shorter, not merely faster.

The live flow

Click through it. Returning (passkey) goes rung 0 → 2 with no email sent — the OS sheet is simulated as a one-second pause, because in the real thing our UI draws nothing at all during it. First sign-in goes 0 → 1 → 2. Any six digits are accepted; type 000000 to see the wrong-code state.

The descent, verified

26 pairs, 0 failures. The first ramp proposed for this brief (neutral-50 → neutral-200 → neutral-800) produced four failures and was discarded rather than shipped. Note that light rung 2 and dark rung 0 are the same surface — the two ramps converge, which is why this is one component and not two.

Every state

The states from §5 of the brief, at the rung each one occurs on, in the selected language and theme. Two are worth looking at hardest: passkey cancelled is not an error and must show no red and no message, and counter regression must fall back silently without ever explaining why on screen.
